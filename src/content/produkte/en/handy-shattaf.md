The Handy Shattaf solves a problem people rarely talk about: water-based intimate hygiene
is a matter of course across much of the world — but on the road, in a van, or in
facilities without fittings it is close to impossible.

The head turns a bottle into a bidet. The jet is directed and metered by hand pressure, so
a small amount of water is enough.

For travellers, for the van, for people with limited mobility — and for organisations
providing people with the essentials.
