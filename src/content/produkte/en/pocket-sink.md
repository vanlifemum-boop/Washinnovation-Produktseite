The Pocket Sink is the counterpart to the other products: it doesn't save water as it
comes out, it saves it by letting you use it twice. What's caught can serve again — hands
first, then dishes.

For anyone staying on pitches or out in nature there's a second point: soapy water does
not belong in the ground or in a stream. A basin is the difference between "tipped out
somewhere" and "disposed of where it belongs".

Folded up, it's as flat as a book.
