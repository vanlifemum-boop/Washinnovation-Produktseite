The CampTap is the smallest piece in the range and the one you'll carry most often. It
turns any bottle into a tap: washing hands before eating, brushing teeth at the roadside,
rinsing sand off your feet.

The jet is the trick. Water poured from an open bottle mostly misses; through the CampTap
it goes where it's needed. That's why a fraction of the usual amount is enough.

With no moving parts, there's nothing to fail. No battery to charge, no pump to prime.
