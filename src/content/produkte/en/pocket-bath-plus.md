The Pocket Bath Plus is the largest set in the range and covers the whole day: showering, washing hands, rinsing dishes, intimate hygiene. Hang the 4-litre bag higher than the shower head and gravity does the rest.

The patented teemer® click valve is the heart of it: press the shower head once to start the stream, press again to stop. Water only runs when it is needed — the actual reason one litre goes so far.

Magnet, suction cup and bracket let you mount the head almost anywhere: on a tailgate, in a shower cubicle, on a tree. The interchangeable nozzles decide whether you get a fine spray or a strong jet.
