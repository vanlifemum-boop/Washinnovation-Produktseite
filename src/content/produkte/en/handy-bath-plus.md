The Handy Bath Plus has the same insides as the Pocket Bath Plus, but works with a self-closing valve: water runs while you hold it and stops by itself when you let go.

The difference in everyday use is the foot pedal. It lets you wash hands or dishes without touching anything — handy when your hands are dirty, and the reason this set is used more often in organisations than the click-valve model.
