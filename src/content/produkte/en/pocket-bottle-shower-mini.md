130 grams, and you bring the water in the bottle you are carrying anyway. The adapter fits most PET bottles from 0.5 to 1.5 litres with openings from 20 to 24.5 millimetres.

That makes this set the opposite of camping gear: it fits in the side pocket of a day pack and is there when water is suddenly needed after a hike, at the beach, or on a night train. The bottle itself is not included.
