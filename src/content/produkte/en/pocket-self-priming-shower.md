This version brings no water bag of its own but a self-priming module. It attaches to whatever is already there: the water canister in the van, the bucket on the pitch, the rain barrel.

It is the lightest way to shower in a van when you carry a canister anyway — 150 grams for head, hose and module. Containers are not included.
