The Handy Shower is for the moment when there is no shower and you need one anyway. After
three days in the van, after the hike, after the festival.

Two litres sounds like nothing. It works because the water doesn't run past you unused:
the jet is fine, the flow can be stopped, and the time between wetting and rinsing costs
you nothing.

You hang it from whatever is there — a branch, a tailgate, a door hook. Folded up, it
disappears into a pocket again.
